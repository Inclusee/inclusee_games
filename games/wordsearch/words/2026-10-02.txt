#  HOW IT WORKS (no coding needed):
#    - One word per line. That is the whole job.

#      same as each other (SEED and SEEDS get confusing to tell apart).
#    - Lines starting with # are notes for us and are never shown to players.
#
#  If a line has a mistake, the game skips that one word and shows a note in
#  the corner saying what to fix. Nothing breaks.
