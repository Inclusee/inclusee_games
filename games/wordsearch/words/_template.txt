#  TIPS FROM THE ACTIVITY TEAM
#    - Use 8 to 12 words. More than that makes the letters small.
#    - Keep words short where you can: 4 to 7 letters is the sweet spot.
#    - Avoid pairs that look alike (SEED / SEEDS, POT / SPOT) - they are hard to
#      tell apart once they are mixed in with filler letters.
#    - Themes work well: seasons, the garden, the kitchen, local places, music,
#      animals, holidays, the news.
