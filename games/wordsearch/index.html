How to play

Tap the first letter of a word, then tap the last letter. If you are right, the word is ticked off and the letters turn green.

You can also drag from the first letter to the last. And on a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move around, then press the space bar to pick the first letter and again for the last.

Words can run backwards as well as forwards on screen, so try both ways round. There is no timer and nothing to lose. Your progress is saved, so you can close the page and come back later.
